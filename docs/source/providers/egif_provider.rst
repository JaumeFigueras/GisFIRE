EGIF provider package
=====================

Constants shared by the EGIF models and the import applications: the provider identity,
the datums and UTM zones the coordinates come in and the CRS each pair means, the two
time zones the wall-clock readings are resolved against, and the cause codes whose
families were established from the published labels.

The module docstring is also where the dataset itself is described — its two export
formats, why both are read, and why a published year is never complete.
